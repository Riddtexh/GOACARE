Your health data, your choice

To save your profile, medical history, prescriptions, appointments and DDSSY claims, GoaCare stores them on its server, linked to your account.

  • Only you can read them, apart from the GoaCare programme administrator, who can view stored account data for support and account checks (prescription photos are not shown to them, and every admin view is logged). Hospital staff see only your name and the reason for an appointment you request with them.
  • Appointment counts (without names) feed the crowd numbers.
  • You can download everything or delete your account and all data at any time, from User Profile.
  • This is a student demo. Do not store information you are not comfortable with.

    Health Insurance

    Estimate a premium and add private cover on top of DDSSY.

    Individual / Family Floater
    One policy for one person, or one shared sum insured for the whole family.
    Senior Citizen Plan
    For 60+, usually with pre-existing disease cover after a waiting period.
    Super Top-up
    Low-cost extra cover that starts after a deductible or after DDSSY is exhausted.
    Critical Illness
    Lump-sum payout on diagnosis of listed conditions such as cancer or heart attack.

    Check before buying: waiting periods, room-rent limits, co-payment, and whether your preferred Goa hospital is in the cashless network. Premiums may qualify for Section 80D deduction. Figures are indicative only, so get an actual quote from a licensed insurer or agent.
