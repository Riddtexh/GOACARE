Health Insurance

Estimate a premium and add private cover on top of DDSSY.

Individual / Family Floater
One policy for one person, or one shared sum insured for the whole family.
Senior Citizen Plan
For 60+, usually with pre-existing disease cover after a waiting period.
Super Top-up
Low-cost extra cover that starts after a deductible or after DDSSY is exhausted.
Critical Illness
Lump-sum payout on diagnosis of listed conditions such as cancer or heart attack.

Check before buying: waiting periods, room-rent limits, co-payment, and whether your preferred Goa hospital is in the cashless network. Premiums may qualify for Section 80D deduction. Figures are indicative only, so get an actual quote from a licensed insurer or agent.

Life Insurance

Estimate term cover and how much protection your family needs.

Term Plan
Pure protection with the highest cover for the lowest premium.
Endowment / Money-back
Insurance plus guaranteed savings, with lower cover per rupee.
Whole Life
Cover for a very long term, often up to age 99 or 100.
ULIP / Pension Plans
Market-linked investment or retirement income combined with cover.

A common rule of thumb is cover of 10 to 15 times annual income. Premiums may qualify for Section 80C, and payouts for Section 10(10D), under current rules. Estimates are indicative only, so get an actual quote from a licensed insurer.
